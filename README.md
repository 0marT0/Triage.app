# Triage.app
Hypothetical Application designed as a medical questionaire for a hypothetical situation.
## Scenario
You are in a Zombie Apocalypse working inside a Hospital as part of their staff. Someone has discovered a vaccine that works as the cure for the Zombie Virus but even then, said cure has a shelf life with each passing day lowering it's potency. This application will help determine if said patient can be cured or must be quarenteed. 
## Triage Rules
Patients are assigned a priority tier. The triage is affected by Age, Hygiene, Medical History, Stage of illness, and Temperature.

- Stages 1–3: Early Infection Phase
The pathogen has entered the body but remains in its initial development. Symptoms are mild, inconsistent, and often mistaken for common illness.
Stage 4: Onset of Systemic Infection
The infection establishes control, spreading rapidly through the host’s body. Physical and neurological symptoms become pronounced and difficult to ignore.
Stage 5: Critical Infection Phase
The pathogen reaches peak influence. The host’s vital systems begin to fail, behavior becomes erratic or aggressive, and recovery is no longer possible.
Stage 6: Full Transformation
The host undergoes complete physiological and cognitive collapse, emerging as a fully transformed entity driven entirely by the infection.

Java 17 or recent
